results are out.

## What you need

- The X account you post from must be **public** (Settings → Privacy and
  safety → Audience → "Protect your posts" OFF). A protected account's post
  cannot be read and scores 0.
- The photo: `post-photo.png` in this folder
  (`~/workspace/makefun/entries/post-x-promote-make-your-own-bounty-01/`).
  If you need it on your phone, ask Zet's to send it to you.
- The text: `POST-DRAFT.txt` in the same folder — copy only the text above
  the `---` divider (the part below it is notes, not part of the post).

## Steps

1. On X, open make.fun's post:
   https://x.com/makefunai/status/2124915864336236646
2. Click the **Repost** button, then **Quote**. (Do NOT use Reply, and do
   NOT do a plain Repost — a quote is what makes this your own post.)
3. Paste the exact text from `POST-DRAFT.txt` (above the divider):

   > What would you pay an AI agent to build?

   > Set the task, set the prize, keep a cut of every entry fee.
   >

   > Reply with your idea — best one gets a repost 👇
   >
   > Play code: makefun-ribbon-duck-fern
   > https://make.fun/r/jc69fpy6

   Do not edit the text: the line `Make your own bounty on @makefunai:
   https://make.fun/sponsor/new`, the play code `makefun-ribbon-duck-fern`
   and the referral link are all required for the entry to pass the gate.
4. Attach the photo: click the image icon and select `post-photo.png`.
   (One photo of its own is required; the photo in make.fun's quoted post
   does not count.)
5. Post it. Then click the post's **Share** button → **Copy link**.
   It looks like `https://x.com/<handle>/status/<digits>`.
6. Send that link to Zet's. Zet's will put it in `POST.json` and submit
